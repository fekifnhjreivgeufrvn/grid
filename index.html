WELCOME TO GRID
1
Import Songs
Click + or drag & drop .osz files onto the song list. Songs are saved in the browser.
2
Pick a Difficulty
Select a song, choose a difficulty from the left panel. Use arrow keys to navigate.
3
How to Play
Notes scroll down as a grid. Press any key or tap when the highlighted cell has notes. Hit PERFECT (22ms), GREAT (65ms), or GOOD (150ms).
4
Scoring
Perfect=100%, Great=50%, Good=30% per note. Grades: A (90%+), S (95%+ no miss), SS (100%).
